# Epistemic Confidence Bands

AION-211 bands bounded confidence as very_low from 0.000000 through 0.200000, low above 0.200000 through 0.400000, medium above 0.400000 through 0.650000, high above 0.650000 through 0.850000, and very_high above 0.850000 through 1.000000.

Confidence is Decimal-based, finite, quantized to six places, and bounded in [0,1]. It is evidence posture confidence, not metaphysical certainty.

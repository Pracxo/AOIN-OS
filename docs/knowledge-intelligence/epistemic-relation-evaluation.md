# Epistemic Relation Evaluation

Correction, retraction, and supersession relations are evaluated from AION-209 graph relation records. The relation itself remains an unverified graph assertion. Correction does not mutate the original claim, retraction does not delete it, and supersession preserves historical claim records.

Applicable retraction forces `retracted`. Applicable supersession without current independent support forces `superseded`. Neither creates automatic correction or retraction effects.

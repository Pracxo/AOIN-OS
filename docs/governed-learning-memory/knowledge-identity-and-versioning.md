# Knowledge Identity and Versioning

Knowledge identity is derived from stable candidate, lineage, scope, and approval references. The identity plan is durable only as a plan during AION-222. It may include append-only version planning, supersession planning, retraction planning, and expiry planning.

AION-222 may produce deterministic fingerprints and version plans in memory. It may not create a persistent knowledge record or mark a claim as durable truth.
